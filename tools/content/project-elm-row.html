01 — The brief

What we found

  • A dark galley kitchen in a 1970s lean-to with a leaking roof
  • The rear wall shared with No. 14, so a party wall award was needed
  • Clay soil and a Victorian drain running under the garden

What we did

  • Took the lean-to down and built a 4.2 m steel-framed extension
  • Agreed the party wall award in week 3, before any digging
  • Re-routed the drain and piled the new footings
  • Kept a working kitchen at the front so the family never moved out
