Why we started

I spent fifteen years as a carpenter on other people’s jobs, and the clients I felt for were the ones left chasing, ringing round on a Thursday to find out nobody had been on site since Monday. When Priya and I started on our own in 2009, we decided ours would never have to chase. We’d write everything down and send it to them every Friday, before they asked.

Tom Hale, co-founder
